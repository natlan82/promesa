Food connects people. We connect food to markets.

We are strategists, designers and project managers who work only with food and beverage brands.

We combine marketing know-how with a real understanding of how food is grown, made, sold and exported. That lets us build plans that work for the producer and the buyer at the same time.

Our focus is extroversion: helping your products reach new shelves in the EU and beyond, with a strategy written for your brand and not taken off a template.
